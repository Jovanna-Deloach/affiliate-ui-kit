# Layout

- **The page well**: `kit-well` is the 1200px (75rem) column with its gutters (`px-gutter`: 1rem, 1.5rem from 768px). Every band of a page sits in it, so every band shares one left edge.
- **Sections** are separated by `mt-chapter`, the density’s section gap (comfortable 80–128px, compact 64–96px, fluid). The first band after the article header uses `mt-12 md:mt-16`; deep dives stack with `gap-10`.
- **Density**: the space inside and between things comes from the density preset (below).
- **Measure**: reading text at most `max-w-measure` (65ch), set with `text-wrap: pretty`; headings are balanced.
- **Spacing** for padding, margin and gap uses the density tokens above or these steps of the 4px scale: 0.5, 1, 1.5, 2, 2.5, 3, 3.5, 4, 5, 6, 8, 10, 12, 16, 20, 24, 32. Lay out with `flex` or `grid` and `gap-*`, never `space-x-*` or `space-y-*`. A part’s `className` is for its placement (margin, grid position, width), never its colors or type.
- **Alignment**: columns that sit side by side start on one line (`items-start`). Bottom alignment (`items-end`, `self-end`, `place-*-end`) is not used; right-align with `text-right` or an auto margin.
- **Container queries**: blocks and components lay out by their own width, not the viewport (`@container`, then `@sm` 24rem, `@md` 28rem, `@xl` 36rem, `@2xl` 42rem, `@3xl` 48rem, `@4xl` 56rem, `@5xl` 64rem), so a block looks right in whatever column it is placed. A phone is a layout, not a squeeze: when width runs out a part drops a word it can spare, never a name or a price.

## Density

The density preset, `data-density` on the page’s root element (Foundations / Themes), sets the space inside and between things. Parts written to the kit’s current rules use these tokens; the rest keep their fixed steps until they move. Touch targets stay at least 44px in both densities.

| Utility | Space | Comfortable | Compact |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| `mt-chapter` | Between a page’s sections | 80–128px, fluid | 64–96px, fluid |
| `px-gutter` | The page’s side margins | 16px, 24px from 768px | 16px, 24px from 768px |
| `p-panel` | Inside a card | 20–32px, fluid | 16–24px, fluid |
| `gap-flow` | Between items in a card or a list | 24px | 16px |
| `px-cell` | A table cell’s sides | 16px | 12px |

# Surfaces

Three layers, never more: **`surface-primary`** (the page), **`surface-raised`** (cards), **`surface-sunken`** (a recess inside a card). The page and the cards are both white: a card stands on the page by its hairline and shadow, never by a tinted page. Containers nest twice at most.

| Utility | What it is |
| --- | --- |
| `kit-card` | `surface-raised` with a 1px `border-subtle` edge, the shape’s `rounded-box` and the theme’s ambient shadow (`--shadow-raised`) |
| `kit-card-feature` | The same white card lifted a step further (`--shadow-featured`): the lead pick’s verdict card only |
| `kit-recess` | `surface-sunken` inside a card, with a `border-bold` edge: the buy box’s offer card |
| `kit-studio` | The radial studio well behind every product photograph |
| `kit-object` | The soft contact shadow under a cut-out product |

Radii are tokens too, set by the shape preset (`data-shape`, Foundations / Themes): `rounded-box` (cards), `rounded-ctl` (controls and pills), `rounded-media` (photographs), and `rounded-full`.

| `data-shape` | `rounded-box` | `rounded-ctl` | `rounded-media` | Default in |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |
| `crisp` | 6px | 4px | 6px | `atelier` |
| `soft` | 8px | 6px | 6px | `assay` |
| `round` | 16px | 12px | 12px | |

Elevation comes only from `kit-card` and `kit-card-feature`, whose shadows the theme sets; no other shadow.

## Interaction and motion

- `kit-focus` gives every interactive element the same focus ring: 2px in the action color, 2px off the edge.
- `kit-link` is the inline link: a thin, low underline that darkens on hover.
- Buttons and retailer rows are at least 44px tall.
- Motion is color only: 180ms on the theme’s easing, cut to 1ms when the reader prefers reduced motion. No scripted animation.
